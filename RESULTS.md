# LexiCore — Benchmark Results

## Environment

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| CPU | x86_64 |
| OS | Ubuntu 24.04, Linux 7.0.0 |
| Compiler | g++ 13.3.0 |
| Build | Release (`-O2`) |
| C++ Standard | C++20 |
| Dictionary | 88,344 unique words (from `/usr/share/dict/words`) |

## Methodology

- **Trials:** 5 per configuration, **median** reported
- **Warm-up:** 1 full pass before timing begins
- **Query set:** 1,000 queries per trial, same across all strategies
- **Fuzzy queries:** generated by random character substitution/insertion/deletion (seeded)
- **Prefix queries:** random prefixes of length 1–5 from dictionary words
- **Dictionary subsets:** seeded random sample (not alphabetical prefix)

## Exact Match: Linear Scan vs Hash Lookup

| Dict Size | Strategy | Total (ms) | Avg (µs) | Queries/sec | p50 (µs) | p95 (µs) | p99 (µs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | Linear scan | 0.86 | 0.86 | 1,164,944 | 0.70 | 1.91 | 2.69 |
| 1,000 | **Hash lookup** | **0.12** | **0.12** | **8,004,482** | 0.09 | 0.12 | 0.13 |
| 10,000 | Linear scan | 12.67 | 12.67 | 78,914 | 11.68 | 28.08 | 31.99 |
| 10,000 | **Hash lookup** | **0.24** | **0.24** | **4,241,134** | 0.12 | 0.46 | 0.98 |
| 88,344 | Linear scan | 121.80 | 121.80 | 8,211 | 112.68 | 262.57 | 298.62 |
| 88,344 | **Hash lookup** | **0.45** | **0.45** | **2,229,674** | 0.37 | 0.76 | 1.04 |

**Observation:** Hash lookup is 7–270× faster than linear scan, with the advantage growing as dictionary size increases. At 88K words, hash achieves 2.2M queries/sec vs. linear's 8.2K.

## Prefix Search: Linear Prefix Scan vs Trie

| Dict Size | Strategy | Total (ms) | Avg (µs) | Queries/sec | p50 (µs) | p95 (µs) | p99 (µs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | Linear prefix scan | 3.64 | 3.64 | 274,403 | 3.77 | 4.54 | 4.82 |
| 1,000 | **Trie autocomplete** | **1.94** | **1.94** | **514,780** | 0.69 | 5.66 | 6.52 |
| 10,000 | Linear prefix scan | 31.78 | 31.78 | 31,470 | 42.08 | 57.91 | 62.98 |
| 10,000 | **Trie autocomplete** | **4.51** | **4.51** | **221,838** | 4.23 | 10.17 | 13.32 |
| 88,344 | Linear prefix scan | 214.71 | 214.71 | 4,657 | 155.48 | 490.19 | 539.36 |
| 88,344 | **Trie autocomplete** | **4.39** | **4.39** | **227,903** | 4.55 | 6.90 | 9.45 |

**Observation:** Trie performance is nearly constant across dictionary sizes (~4.4 µs at both 10K and 88K), confirming O(k) complexity independent of dictionary size. Linear scan degrades linearly. At 88K words, trie is ~49× faster.

## Fuzzy Search: Linear Edit-Distance Scan vs BK-Tree (maxDist=2)

| Dict Size | Strategy | Total (ms) | Avg (µs) | Queries/sec | p50 (µs) | p95 (µs) | p99 (µs) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 | Linear edit-dist scan | 163.58 | 163.58 | 6,113 | 162.56 | 225.69 | 263.92 |
| 1,000 | **BK-tree search** | **82.71** | **82.71** | **12,090** | 86.97 | 107.40 | 111.83 |
| 10,000 | Linear edit-dist scan | 1,639.65 | 1,639.65 | 610 | 1,631.69 | 2,253.99 | 2,503.08 |
| 10,000 | **BK-tree search** | **663.81** | **663.81** | **1,506** | 707.31 | 918.16 | 959.95 |
| 88,344 | Linear edit-dist scan | 13,818.35 | 13,818.35 | 72 | 13,690.35 | 19,306.04 | 21,599.44 |
| 88,344 | **BK-tree search** | **5,729.39** | **5,729.39** | **175** | 6,277.52 | 8,240.14 | 8,797.34 |

**Observation:** BK-tree is consistently ~2–2.5× faster than linear scan across all dictionary sizes, with triangle-inequality pruning avoiding a significant fraction of edit-distance computations. The advantage holds at scale — at 88K words, BK-tree averages 5.7ms per query vs. linear's 13.8ms.

---

## Summary

| Query Type | Winner | Speedup at 88K |
|---|---|---|
| Exact match | Hash lookup | **271×** faster |
| Prefix search | Trie | **49×** faster |
| Fuzzy search | BK-tree | **2.4×** faster |

The results confirm that data structure choice matters — and matters differently depending on query type and dictionary scale. The trie's O(k) behavior is particularly striking: its latency barely changes from 10K to 88K words, while linear scan degrades proportionally.
